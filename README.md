### Hi there, I'm MAHDI HASAN👋 

### PhD Student | Space Systems & Microelectronics Reliability Enthusiast

🎓 I am currently pursuing a **Ph.D. in Intelligent Systems Engineering** at **Indiana University Bloomington, USA**, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant at the **HERMES Lab** under **Center for Reliable and Trusted Electronics**. My research focuses on **radiation effects in microelectronics, pulsed-laser single-event effects (SEE), and semiconductor reliability in extreme environments**.

I earned my **B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)** from **Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), Bangladesh**. I also completed academic training in **Remote Sensing, GIS, and GNSS** at the **Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS), ISRO**.

My research interests span **Spacecraft Systems Engineering, CubeSat Development, Radiation-Tolerant Electronics, Space Robotics, and Intelligent Space Systems**.

---

## Current Research Activities

🔵 **PhD Research | Indiana University Bloomington**

As a research assistant at the HERMES Laboratory, my work involves:

- **Single-Event Effects (SEE):** Radiation-induced disturbances in semiconductor devices and electronic circuits.
- **Pulsed-Laser SEE Testing:** Laser-based characterization of microelectronics under simulated radiation-induced conditions.
- **Device-to-Circuit Modeling:** Understanding the propagation of radiation-induced effects from devices to circuits.
- **Experimental Research:** Particle accelerator-related testing, detector electronics, and experimental characterization.

🔵 **Spacecraft and CubeSat Systems Engineering**

Alongside my doctoral research, I am interested in applying radiation-effects research to **CubeSat subsystems, spacecraft avionics, onboard computers, electrical power systems, fault-tolerant electronics, and mission reliability**.

## Previous Research Experience

🔵 **AUSTSAT Nanosatellite Development | Research Assistant & Team Lead**

At the **Satellite Communication Laboratory (SCL), AUST**, I contributed to the AUSTSAT Nanosatellite Program, coordinating eight subsystem teams and working on **CubeSat engineering models, onboard computers (OBC), electrical power systems (EPS), satellite communication, PCB design, subsystem integration, and testing**.

🔵 **Sounding Model Rocket | Research & Team Lead**

Led the development of a subsonic sounding model rocket, focusing on **flight-control systems (PID/MPC), avionics, telemetry, sensor integration, trajectory simulation, and non-pyrotechnic recovery systems**, using SolidWorks, ANSYS, and OpenRocket.

🔵 **Remote Sensing & Artificial Intelligence Research**

Conducted research on **environmental monitoring of the Sundarbans** using satellite imagery, NDVI, NDWI, and Random Forest machine learning classification, resulting in an IEEE conference publication.
